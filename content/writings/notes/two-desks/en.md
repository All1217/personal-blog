At four the rain stops being a reminder to take the laundry in and becomes a fact. The Chinese draft sits on the left, the English one on the right. A cup keeps them from collapsing into each other.

## Start with what the window did

Rain is not the subject. It is the tempo. In Chinese I reach for “too late” first. In English I notice why the glass suddenly looks lit: each drop turns the pane into a temporary lamp.

![Two drafts](./cover.svg)

The same cup is “still warm” in one language and `still warm enough to hold` in the other. Close in meaning, different in where the sentence lands.

## The table of contents should switch too

Change the language and the title, the excerpt, this paragraph, and the headings in the sidebar should all belong to this desk. The URL can stay put. You have not left the room. You only reached for the other stack of paper.

## Then take a sip

Do not sand the two drafts into one another. If both of them remember the same rain, that is enough.
